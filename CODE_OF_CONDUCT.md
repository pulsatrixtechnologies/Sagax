# Code of Conduct

Sagax follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1, which is adopted here by reference. It applies in the Sagax
repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, commits, reviews) and in any
space where someone represents the project.

The short version:

- Be respectful and constructive. Critique code, not people.
- No harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks, in issues, PRs, commits, or anywhere else in
  the project's spaces.
- Assume good faith, especially with first-time contributors, and remember maintainers are people
  with finite time.

## Enforcement

Unacceptable behavior can be reported privately to **conduct@pulsatrix.ca**, or by contacting a
maintainer of [pulsatrixtechnologies/sagax](https://github.com/pulsatrixtechnologies/sagax)
directly. Reports are handled confidentially, and the reporter's identity is not shared without
consent. Pulsatrix Technologies inc. maintains Sagax and is responsible for enforcement.

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and comments that violate this code, and may
warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently ban a person from the project's spaces, following the
enforcement guidelines of the Covenant 2.1. A conduct report about a maintainer goes to the
address above and is handled by another maintainer.
