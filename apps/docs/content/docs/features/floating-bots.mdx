## Put a bot on the desktop

Right-click a bot in the sidebar and choose **Put on the desktop**. The bot leaves the window and stands on your desktop as its mascot, above your other apps. In a browser or on a phone it floats over the app instead. Choose **Put back in the window** from its right-click menu to bring it home.

## Choose its character

Each bot picks its own character, in two places that stay in sync:

- the **Mascot** tab of the mascot's balloon (double-click it, then **Mascot**), or **Mascot** in its right-click menu;
- **Character** at the top of the bot's avatar popover (click the big avatar in the bot's panel).

The choices are the **Owl** (the Sagax owl, as in the app), the **Original shapes** (with a choice of shape) and **Trombi**. The owl can also be shown in **3D (preview)**; the flat owl stays the default. A bot's own picture, if it has one, rides along as a small medallion. The popover only offers what the chosen character supports: the owl keeps its skins and wing moves, the others show a preview of their own moves.

## Talk to it

- **Double-click** the mascot, or press **Enter** while it has focus, to open its balloon and send it a message. The reply streams into the balloon; **Open in Sagax** jumps to the full thread.
- **Right-click** it for its menu: open the thread, show or hide the balloon, always on top, fly away during tasks, activity level, character, or put it back in the window.
- **Drag** it anywhere: it flaps and kicks while you carry it and lands with a wobble.

## Play with it

The mascot has a little life of its own: it breathes and blinks, follows your pointer with its eyes, looks around, preens, scratches, pecks, stretches a wing, hoots, walks a few steps along the desk (turning to face the way it goes) and now and then hops or flies a short way. After a quiet while it yawns and naps until you come back.

- **Click** it: a joyful move (a wave, a dance, an excited jump, a cuddle). Too many clicks in a row make it cross.
- **Stroke** it by moving the pointer back and forth over it: it hugs itself and leans into your hand; keep going and it shows hearts.
- Rush the pointer past it and it jumps; rest the pointer near it and it waves.

The bar under the mascot is its energy: how much of the model's context its current thread has left, the same figure as the ring in the chat header. It drains as the conversation grows, turns amber past half and red near the end. Hover it to read the percentage.

## Fly away during tasks

When the bot starts working, the mascot crouches, spreads its wings and flies to the nearest edge of its screen, flapping all the way, and waits there as a small badge with a turning ring. Click the badge to open the thread. When the work is done it flies back to the spot it left, lands and celebrates; if a message could not be sent it comes back looking sad, and if the bot needs your approval it comes straight back.

Turn this off in **App settings → Appearance → Fly away during tasks**, or from the mascot's right-click menu. With it off, the mascot stays put and thinks while the bot works.

## Activity level

**App settings → Appearance → Activity level** (also in the right-click menu) sets how often the mascot moves on its own: **Calm**, **Normal** or **Lively**.

## Motion and battery

- With **Reduce motion** on in your system settings, the mascot stays calm: no walks, flights or big moves, only breathing, blinking and gentle looks.
- It draws only as fast as it needs, and not at all while hidden or covered.
- Clicks pass through the empty space around the mascot to whatever is underneath.
