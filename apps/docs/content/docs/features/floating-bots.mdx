## Put a bot on the desktop

Right-click a bot in the sidebar and choose **Put on the desktop**. The bot leaves the window and stands on your desktop as a small 3D owl in its own color, above your other apps. In a browser or on a phone it floats over the app instead. Choose **Put back in the window** from its right-click menu to bring it home.

A bot with its own picture keeps that picture on the desktop; every other bot is the owl.

## Talk to it

- **Double-click** the owl, or press **Enter** while it has focus, to open its speech balloon and send it a message. The reply streams into the balloon; **Open in Sagax** jumps to the full thread.
- **Right-click** it for its menu: open the thread, show or hide the balloon, keep it always on top, fly away during tasks, or put it back in the window.
- **Drag** it anywhere. It flaps its wings while you carry it and remembers where you dropped it, per screen setup.

## Play with it

The owl has a little life of its own. It breathes, blinks, follows your pointer with its head, looks around, spins on itself, hops, and now and then walks a few steps along the desk. After a couple of quiet minutes it naps (watch for the Zzz) until you come back.

- **Click** it once: it spins with joy and sends up hearts.
- **Stroke** it by moving the pointer back and forth over it: it leans into your hand and purrs.
- **Hover** it to see its mood meter. Playing, stroking, and finished tasks raise the mood; it drifts down slowly when nobody plays, never below a calm floor. Nothing bad ever happens to a lonely owl: it only gets a little sleepy.

The mood is kept per bot on this device only.

## Fly away during tasks

When the bot starts working, the owl takes off and flies to the nearest edge of its screen, where it waits as a small owl badge with a turning ring. Click the badge to open the thread. When the work is done it flies back to the exact spot it left and celebrates; if a message could not be sent it comes back looking sad, and if the bot needs your approval it comes straight back so you can answer.

Turn this off in **App settings → Appearance → Fly away during tasks**, or from the owl's own right-click menu. With it off, the owl stays put and ponders while the bot works.

## Motion and battery

- With **Reduce motion** on in your system settings, the owl stays calm: no spins, hops, walks, or flights, only breathing, blinking, and gentle looks.
- The owl draws only as fast as it needs: smoothly while it moves, slower at rest, slower still asleep, and not at all while hidden or covered.
- Clicks pass through the empty space around the owl to whatever is underneath.
- Without WebGL (some virtual machines and remote sessions), the owl falls back to the flat 2D owl with the same behavior.
