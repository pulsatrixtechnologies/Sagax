## Put a bot on the desktop

Right-click a bot in the sidebar and choose **Put on the desktop**. The bot leaves the window and stands on your desktop as its mascot, above your other apps. In a browser or on a phone it floats over the app instead. Choose **Put back in the window** from its right-click menu to bring it home.

## Choose its character

Each bot picks its character in its avatar popover (click the big avatar in the bot's panel): the **Owl** (with its colors, skins and a 2D or 3D preview style), one of the thirteen **Shapes** (circle, cloud, rounded square, sparkle, clover, bean, flower, drop, pill, pick, house, star, hexagon, all with the same face, a color and a skin), **Trombi** or **Bunbu** (our own little collectible monster with long ears and a toothy grin, in the bot's color and a Bunbu skin; its signature move is the ear flop). The big avatar above the popover is the preview: it plays the moves listed at the bottom and the equip animation of a skin you pick. The choice is stored with the bot, so it shows everywhere the bot appears (the sidebar, the chat, the panels, the desktop mascot) and on your other devices.

Skins come in four rarities, one tab each in the popover (with how many skins it holds), and the popover opens on the tab of the current skin: Common (Owl: classic, snowy, barn, carbon; Shapes: plain, pastel, glossy, night; Trombi: classic, retro 98; Bunbu: plain, pastel, night, plush), Rare (Owl: gold, ice; Shapes: outline, gold; Trombi: gold; Bunbu: velvet, gold), Epic (Owl: neon, lightning, chrome; Shapes: neon, chrome, crystal, circuit; Trombi: neon, chrome, glitch; Bunbu: neon, chrome, crystal) and Legendary (Owl: inferno, holographic, galaxy, spirit; Shapes: holographic, molten, galaxy; Trombi: holographic, molten; Bunbu: holographic, galaxy, molten). The iPhone app shows a Bunbu bot as the owl for now. A premium skin has an idle effect, an equip animation and its own effects on every move (trails, particles, shockwaves). Small avatars such as the sidebar show its still look; with Reduce motion on, every skin keeps its look and nothing moves or flashes. Each character keeps its own skin when you switch and come back.

Colors come in five palettes, one tab each: Vivid (the original bright colors), Pastel, Deep, Neon and Neutral (white, greys, black, sand, browns and metals). The popover opens on the palette of the bot's color, and a bot keeps its color from any earlier version. Every color keeps the eyes readable. The Shape grid previews each shape in the current color and skin.

## Talk to it

- **Click** the mascot, or press **Enter** while it has focus, to open its balloon, ready to type. The reply streams into the balloon; **Open in Sagax** jumps to the full thread.
- **Double-click** it to open Sagax on the bot's thread.
- The balloon grows with the answer (up to about 60 % of the screen, then it scrolls, earlier exchanges above), shows lists, bold, code, tables and links like the chat, and opens below or to the right when the mascot is near the top or the left edge. Drag its corner grip to resize it, drag its header to move it anywhere, right up against the mascot (it stops before covering its face), and use the pin button to put it back; its size and place are remembered per bot. **Enter** sends, **Shift+Enter** starts a new line, **Esc** closes it. With Trombi it wears the Windows 98 look.
- **Call** it: where voice mode is on for the bot, the phone button in the balloon's header (or **Call** in the right-click menu) starts a voice call through the bot's thread, the same call as in the app. A small call pill stands under the mascot: the live waveform, Settings, Transcript, Mic and the red button to hang up. The mascot bounces while its bot talks and leans in while you speak (not with Reduce motion on); click it to cut the bot short. Only one call runs at a time: starting one in the app or on another mascot ends the other.
- **Right-click** it for its menu: open the thread, show or hide the balloon, always on top, fly away during tasks, activity level, or put it back in the window.
- **Drag** it anywhere: it flaps and kicks while you carry it and lands with a wobble.

## Play with it

The mascot has a little life of its own: it breathes and blinks, follows your pointer with its eyes, looks around, preens, scratches, pecks, stretches a wing, hoots, walks a few steps along the desk (turning to face the way it goes) and now and then hops or flies a short way. After a quiet while it yawns and naps until you come back.

- **Stroke** it by moving the pointer back and forth over it: it hugs itself and leans into your hand; keep going and it shows hearts.
- Rush the pointer past it and it jumps; rest the pointer near it and it waves.

## Fly away during tasks

When the bot starts working, the mascot crouches, spreads its wings and flies to the nearest edge of its screen, flapping all the way, and waits there as a small badge with a turning ring. Click the badge to open the thread. When the work is done it flies back to the spot it left, lands and celebrates; if a message could not be sent it comes back looking sad, and if the bot needs your approval it comes straight back.

Turn this off in **App settings → Appearance → Fly away during tasks**, or from the mascot's right-click menu. With it off, the mascot stays put and thinks while the bot works.

## Activity level

**App settings → Appearance → Activity level** (also in the right-click menu) sets how often the mascot moves on its own: **Calm**, **Normal** or **Lively**.

## Motion and battery

- With **Reduce motion** on in your system settings, the mascot stays calm: no walks, flights or big moves, only breathing, blinking and gentle looks.
- It draws only as fast as it needs, and not at all while hidden or covered.
- Clicks pass through the empty space around the mascot to whatever is underneath.
