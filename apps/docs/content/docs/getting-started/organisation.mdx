Sagax at work is your organization's Perspicax server. There is no hosted Admin portal, no Company model catalog, and no company cloud backup.

<Callout type="info" title="What stays yours">
  Joining does not upload this computer's chats unless you choose specific bots to copy. Your own engines, accounts and local models stay on this computer.
</Callout>

## Join a Perspicax server

1. Open **Settings → Organization**.
2. Under **Join a Perspicax server**, enter the server address your administrator gave you.
3. Optionally choose which of your bots to copy, with or without their conversations and memory. You can preview the copy before you join.
4. Choose **Join**. The desktop opens that server and starts **Sign in with Pulsatrix**.

On a server that is already signed in with Perspicax, Settings → Organization shows the organization, your role, and a link to manage it in the Perspicax console. People, models and billing for the organization are managed there, not in a separate Admin portal.

## A shared installation

Some organizations give you a link to a shared installation instead of the desktop app. It runs in your browser. Sign in through the invitation your administrator sent. Bots, chats and files on that installation are shared with your team.
