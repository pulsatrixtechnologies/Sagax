speaker-*.wav are the first three seconds of the speaker recognition test
recordings published with sherpa-onnx (k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx, release
"speaker-recongition-models": fangjun-sr-1, fangjun-sr-2, fangjun-test-sr-1 as
speaker-a; leijun-sr-1, leijun-test-sr-1 as speaker-b; liudehua-sr-1,
liudehua-test-sr-1 as speaker-c), used under the Apache License 2.0
(third_party/sherpa-onnx/LICENSE). They are test fixtures only and are not
part of the built app.

fbank-kaldi.json holds reference filter bank rows computed with
kaldi-native-fbank (Apache License 2.0) from speaker-a-1.wav.

Noise, music, keyboard, TV and echo fixtures are synthesized by the tests
(vad.test.ts) from these recordings and from generated signals.
